---
name: Packyo curated saves
description: Product intent for profile activities and stays, Discover saving, and reuse in new trips.
---

Profile Activities and Stays are explicitly curated saves from Discover, not an automatic archive of wishes, votes, itinerary picks, or completed accommodations.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested replacing the old automatic lists and removing automatic population from voted activities. Original trip wishes and voting history should not be deleted to clear those derived lists.

**How to apply:** New saved-item entry points should require an explicit save. Reusing a save in a matching destination creates a normal wish or accommodation suggestion under the current traveller's identity, subject to the same group vote as other suggestions. Never pre-vote or silently select a saved item.

Accommodation ties must remain visible as tied winners until the trip creator explicitly requests Packyo's tiebreak. Booking is a separate action after resolution.

**Why:** The user does not want the first tied option or an automatic AI decision presented as the sole winner.

**How to apply:** Invalidate a tiebreak when the ballot or participation changes; show every tied leader before resolution.
